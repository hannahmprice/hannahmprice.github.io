abstract: "We explore mean-field ground states of coupled one-dimensional Bose-Einstein condensates in a synthetic magnetic field. For two wires, finite longitudinal size modifies transitions between vortex-like, biased-density and Meissner-like states. With more wires, extended vortex lattices emerge. Boundary conditions in the discrete direction affect the arrangement of vortices, connecting few-leg ladder physics with vortex lattices in continuous-discrete quantum fluids."
