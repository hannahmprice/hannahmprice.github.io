Postdoctoral Research Fellow, University of Birmingham  <br/> Started 2025.

I am interested in the theoretical exploration of quantum many-body simulators with large local Hilbert spaces, focusing on how dimensionality, geometry, and exotic quantum statistics shape their behavior. These systems offer a rich landscape for discovering novel emergent particles and advancing quantum engineering. Unlike spin-1/2 systems, the added complexity of a large but finite local Hilbert space introduces unique quantum phenomena. Platforms such as Rydberg atoms and polar molecules make these ideas experimentally viable, combining intricate internal structures with long-range interactions—an ideal setting to investigate topological order and long-range entangled states. My approach emphasizes a strong dialogue between theory and experiment: learning from experimental insights to refine models and, in turn, guiding experimental design with informed predictions of key parameters.

Past. Aug 2025 [PhD, Rice University, Houston, TX USA] – Title: “Collective quantum phenomena in strongly correlated multi-level systems” – Supervisor: Dr. Kaden Hazzard
