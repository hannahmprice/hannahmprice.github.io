My current research interests include exploring the effects of topology in quantum mechanical systems, using Floquet engineering to produce experimentally feasible models with gauge fields, and investigating models that involve Non-Abelian lattice gauge fields. 

I previously completed my MSci in Theoretical Physics at the University of Birmingham. During my master's project, I explored the infinite U Fermi-Hubbard model on the Pyrochlore lattice.
