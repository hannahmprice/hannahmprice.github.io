abstract: "We demonstrate that winding of complex momentum bands can enforce localization at temporal interfaces, revealing topology hidden in the usual energy-band description. Photonic quantum walks allow experimental tests of this effect and its response to disorder. The observed topology survives arbitrarily strong disorder within the studied setting; extreme spatiotemporally random non-Hermiticity provides a route to its destruction."
